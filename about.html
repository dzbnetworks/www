DZB Networks exists for one reason: serious cybersecurity shouldn't be a privilege reserved for the Fortune 500. We bring enterprise-grade defense to the businesses that keep the economy running.

Our story

Founded by people who'd seen the breaches

DZB Networks started after our founders spent years cleaning up preventable incidents — ransomware that walked through a flat network, breaches traced back to a single unpatched server, businesses brought to a standstill because nobody was watching at 2am.

We built DZB to flip that script: to be the team that's already inside the building, watching, hardening and ready — so the breach simply doesn't happen. Today we protect hundreds of endpoints across finance, healthcare, SaaS and the public sector, and we treat every client's network as if it were our own.
